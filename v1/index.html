FAQ

Questions, answered.

Choosing a home care provider is a big decision. These answers can help you feel confident about it.

What services do you offer?

Personal care assistance, meal preparation, medication reminders, light housekeeping, companionship, and specialized care like dementia support and palliative care.

How are caregivers selected?

Our caregivers go through a rigorous screening process, including background checks, thorough interviews, and professional training. We also match caregivers based on each client’s needs and preferences.

Do you offer customized care plans?

Yes. We design personalized care plans around each client’s needs, goals, and lifestyle, and we regularly review and adjust them to ensure the best care possible.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Montgomery, Philadelphia, Bucks, Chester, and Delaware counties.

Is there a minimum number of hours required?

No fixed minimum is listed. We offer flexible options, including hourly, daily, and live-in care. Contact us to discuss a schedule that works for you.

Can I make changes to the care plan?

Absolutely. We encourage open communication and are happy to adjust the plan as your needs evolve.
