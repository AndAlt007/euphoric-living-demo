FAQ

Got questions? Good.

What services do you offer?

Personal care assistance, meal preparation, medication reminders, light housekeeping, companionship, and specialized care like dementia support and palliative care.

How are caregivers selected?

A rigorous screening process, including background checks, thorough interviews, and professional training. We also match caregivers to each client’s needs and preferences.

Do you offer customized care plans?

Yes. Every plan is designed around the client’s needs, goals, and lifestyle, and regularly reviewed and adjusted.

What areas do you serve?

Montgomery, Philadelphia, Bucks, Chester, and Delaware counties.

Is there a minimum number of hours?

No fixed minimum is listed. We offer hourly, daily, and live-in care. Contact us to find a schedule that works.

Can I change the care plan?

Absolutely. We encourage open communication and adjust the plan as your needs evolve.
